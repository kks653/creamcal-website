Simplify Your Schedule with CreamCal

CreamCal is a personal productivity tool designed to help you organize your life.
Seamlessly integrate with Google Calendar to view, edit, and manage your events.

Purpose of the Application

Why we built CreamCal and how it helps you.

📅 Comprehensive Event Management

CreamCal allows users to create, read, update, and delete events directly on their Google Calendar. Our goal is to provide a more intuitive and focused interface for managing daily tasks and schedules without distractions.

🔄 Real-time Synchronization

The app requires access to your Google Calendar to ensure that any changes you make in CreamCal are instantly reflected across all your devices linked to your Google account.

🔒 Data Privacy First

We value your privacy. CreamCal processes your calendar data locally on your device to display your schedule. We do not store your personal data on external servers.

Key Features

View Events

Check your daily, weekly, and monthly schedules at a glance.

Edit Schedules

Easily modify event details, times, and descriptions.

Direct Sync

All actions are performed directly via the official Google Calendar API.
